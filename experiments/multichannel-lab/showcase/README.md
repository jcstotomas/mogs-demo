# Make room for good work

A coordinated fictional MOGS campaign for the isolated multichannel lab: two HTML emails, a five-slide sales deck, and three photographic creatives. The campaign uses forest green, warm ivory, citrus accents, serif headlines, and quieter supporting typography. It is a separate visual-demo collection, not part of the 22-asset launch inventory or the frozen 12-asset regression corpus.

| Asset | File | Context |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome email | [HTML](emails/welcome-room-to-work.html) | New customers; stale monthly price and unchanged annual price |
| Subscriber email | [HTML](emails/your-familiar-rate.html) | Eligible existing customers; preserve legacy pricing |
| Sales deck | [PowerPoint](deck/files/mogs-make-room.pptx), [PDF](deck/files/mogs-make-room.pdf) | Five slides with direct and derived price claims |
| Launch creative | [PNG](creative/mogs-launch.png) | New customers; stale monthly price |
| Annual creative | [PNG](creative/mogs-annual.png) | Stale savings percentage; unchanged annual price |
| Subscriber creative | [PNG](creative/mogs-legacy.png) | Eligible existing customers; protected monthly price |

The built-in **Load designed campaign** action imports these six files using the contexts and hashes in [manifest.json](manifest.json). Pricing intentionally represents the source campaign before the $30-to-$40 Starter change. The campaign assets themselves are not rewritten by analysis. The deck's original text remains editable in PowerPoint; raster creative copy has no native design layers.

The hero and three creatives are original image-generation outputs. [image-generation.json](image-generation.json) records the exact prompts and generation mode. The text-free hero is embedded locally in the email and deck. There are no externally loaded image dependencies. The original generated files were preserved in Codex's generated-images folder as well.

## Verification and limits

The final collection has 17 declared pricing occurrences, all matched by the real parsers/OCR and deterministic rules. Eight checked substitutions are proposed. All 123 extracted blocks receive findings. The original regression collection remains unchanged and passes all 65 declared occurrences; 66 automated tests and the lab build pass.

These passing expectations do not mean complete semantic coverage. The campaign report remains partial: image regions are not fully understood, two standalone deck claims are withheld, two general pricing phrases remain unresolved, and one false OCR price-like region remains visible. No unsupported phrase or uncertain visual region is silently treated as verified. Email photography and the deck cover remain explicit extraction omissions.

See [campaign verification](../evidence/CAMPAIGN_DESIGN.md) for named runs, browser evidence, review findings, and the before/after comparison. No assets were sent, published, or added to the core correction inventory. The prior local library remains preserved under its original storage name; the current demo uses `campaign-review-final`.

## Rebuilding

The email and deck generators are in `../scripts/build-showcase-emails.mjs` and `../scripts/build-showcase-deck.mjs`. The deck generator uses the bundled artifact runtime and bundled LibreOffice. Generated imagery is an input, not regenerated by those scripts. For a future redesign, use a new collection version and review expectations before evaluation; preserve this named evidence and frozen source hashes.
